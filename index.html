The basics

How It Works

OMG doesn't replace your hiring process; it changes where the filter sits. Every applicant takes a sales-specific assessment before anyone spends an hour interviewing them. The assessment is configured to your selling environment — deal size, who they're calling on, market — so "Recommended" means recommended for this role at Hubexo, not sales-capable in general.

Recommended

Move them forward — the assessment earns them the conversation, not the offer.

Not recommended

Do not interview. Salespeople interview for a living — the ones who'd charm you in the room are exactly the ones the data exists to protect you from.

5
Act fast

5-minute phone screen

Schedule it the moment a Recommended comes back — good candidates don't stay on the market.

6

Interview 1 — they prove to you

Put as much pressure on them as they'll experience in the role, and look for evidence, not words. If the role involves cold calling, roleplay a live cold call. Lushin supplies interview questions built from each candidate's report.

7

Interview 2 — get on the same page

Compensation, expectations, logistics — the alignment conversation.

8

Offer letter

Marking the hire in the portal also unlocks a complimentary onboarding upgrade — two coaching reports plus a strengths video for their new manager.

90-day onboarding plan

The coaching reports from step 8 feed straight into the ramp plan.
